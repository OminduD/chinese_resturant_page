Our Story

✦

Three generations of culinary mastery — honoring ancient recipes with a modern spirit.

Golden Dragon was born from a vision shared by Master Chef Wei Long and his grandmother — to bring the authentic, uncompromising flavors of China to a world that yearned for something real. In 1985, a modest shopfront in Chinatown opened its doors with just twelve tables and a single wok. The aromas of Sichuan peppercorns and hand-pulled noodles drew visitors from across the city.

Today, nearly four decades later, that same dedication to craft endures. Every dish is prepared using time-honored techniques passed down through three generations. We source the finest ingredients — from aged soy sauces brewed in clay urns to vegetables harvested at dawn from local farms.

The Golden Dragon experience is not merely a meal. It is a journey through China's rich culinary landscapes: the fiery boldness of Sichuan, the delicate refinement of Cantonese dim sum, the hearty warmth of Northern wheat noodles, and the subtle artistry of Shanghainese cooking.

Our Philosophy

烹饪哲学 · The Way of the Wok

🔥 火候 · Mastery of Fire

Wok hei — the breath of the wok — is the soul of Chinese cooking. Our chefs command flames exceeding 1,200°F to sear flavors in seconds, locking in freshness and creating that unmistakable smoky essence.

🌿 新鲜 · Fresh First

We believe great food begins with exceptional ingredients. Daily deliveries of the freshest produce, sustainably sourced seafood, and premium meats form the foundation of every Golden Dragon creation.

⚖️ 平衡 · Balance

Chinese cuisine is an orchestra of five flavors — sweet, sour, bitter, salty, and umami. Our chefs harmonize these elements meticulously, ensuring every bite tells a complete and satisfying story.
